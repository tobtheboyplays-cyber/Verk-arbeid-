# Coins and your first workers

Founding creates a Mayor and three available settlers. The Mayor leads the
settlement; choose one of the other three for your first job.

## First Lumberer

1. Establish a valid Hearth and complete the visible **Foundation Ready**
   objective so **Timber Rights** can be purchased.
2. Sell player-gathered **Basic** logs to the visiting merchant. Ordinary
   player-gathered goods are valid sales goods; no worker or rare tool is
   required for the bootstrap.
3. A Basic timber shipment costs **8 logs for 1 Coin**. The first four Timber
   sales at an active visitor use that base quote. The visitor's whole market
   shares an 8-Coin purse on the first visit, so every row and player draws
   from the same remaining budget.
4. Buy **Timber Rights** for 2 Coins, then build and register a valid Lumber
   Camp and set its work zone.
5. Buy a **Lumberer Emblem** for 1 Coin. Give it to one settler and provide a
   real axe through the Lumber Camp. The worker needs a durable axe before
   they can cut trees.

Three Basic log shipments cost 24 logs and fund the 3-Coin first worker path.
Coins are physical items. They can be spent from your inventory, the Hearth,
or registered Warehouse storage.

## Build the village economy

A real Lumberer delivery makes **Stores and Roads** available. Buy it, hire a
Courier there, and complete a real delivery to make **Cultivated Ground**
available. **Home** is a separate 2-Coin foundation path: it requires Shelter
and Foundation Ready, not a stored Farmer crop. Home unlocks houses and
lodging; Hospitality then requires Home and three housed settlers before it
can register a Tavern.

Recruitment needs a free bed, sufficient morale, prepared-food reserve, and
the exact Coin price shown for that traveler. Aptitude and earned discounts can
change the quote. A valid Tavern also needs its Plaque requirements, including
the connected Ale Tap and barrel, before it can support normal service.

## Earn more without a Coin farm

Each merchant row is finite, and the visitor's shared purse is the hard
market-wide cap. A visitor remains for up to 20 minutes; the same settlement
can receive its next eligible visitor every 20 minutes. Later visitors carry a
12-Coin shared purse. Reopening the menu, reloading, killing/replacing the
visitor, changing wood species, or adding a second player never refills it.

Quality improves efficiency in small steps:

| Quality | Timber, iron, honey | Field goods |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Basic | 8 per Coin | 16 per Coin |
| Fine | 7 per Coin | 14 per Coin |
| Superior | 6 per Coin | 12 per Coin |
| Exceptional | 5 per Coin | 10 per Coin |
| Masterwork | 4 per Coin | 9 per Coin |
| Legendary | 4 per Coin | 8 per Coin |

Basic, Fine and Superior rows hold up to four Coin sales. Exceptional and
Masterwork hold two; Legendary holds one. After the fourth active sale in a
Timber, Field, or Specialty family, all remaining rows in that family take one
25% rounded-up input increase. Read the live offer before trading.

The Tavern earns Coins only from real completed guest service, and its guests
have finite purses. Raid rewards and goblin theft are separate event systems;
they should shape recovery and tension, not replace gathering and trade.
