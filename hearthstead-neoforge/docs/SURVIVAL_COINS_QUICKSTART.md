# From your first Hearth to your first worker

**1. Found a village.** Craft a Hearth with three logs, five cobblestone and a
campfire. Place it on open, dry ground with clear walking space around it.
Three available workers and a dedicated Mayor arrive. Keep food in the Hearth
and stay nearby while the village gets established.

**2. Earn the first Coins.** Watch for “A merchant is approaching.” The
merchant walks in from outside the village; a usable route is needed. Carry
logs, approach the wandering merchant and right-click to open the ordinary
Minecraft trade window.

Ordinary player-gathered logs are **Basic**. A Basic timber shipment costs
**8 logs for 1 Coin**. The first four active Timber sales use that base quote;
after the fourth sale, remaining Timber rows take one 25% rounded-up input
increase. The first visitor's whole market shares an 8-Coin purse; later
visitors share 12. A row's use count is not a separate Coin allowance, and
reopening, reloading, changing wood type or trading with a friend cannot
refill the purse.

**3. Pay for your first worker.** At the Hearth, complete the displayed
Foundation Ready objective, then open **Growth** and learn **Timber Rights —
2 Coins**. Build and register a valid Lumber Camp using its plan and Plaque.

With your main hand empty, right-click your Mayor to open **Job Emblems**. Buy
a **Lumberer Emblem — 1 Coin**. Hold the emblem and right-click an available
original worker **without sneaking**. A valid assignment consumes the emblem;
the Mayor stays Mayor. There is no separate Hire button.

**4. Make the camp work.** Use a Work Scepter on its Plaque and mark a
three-dimensional zone containing a real tree. Supply an accepted physical axe
in the worker’s inventory or camp storage. Check the worker’s need icon and
**Hearth → Tasks** if work stalls. Empty both hands and Shift-right-click a
worker to open their inventory. Let the Lumberer fell, collect and deliver a
log to the camp chest: this actual delivery advances development.

**5. Keep earning and grow.** Player-gathered goods are Basic. Actual worker
output starts at Fine, with higher grades earned through completed work, skill,
equipment and a valid workplace. The current live quote is shown by the
merchant: timber/iron/honey require 8 / 7 / 6 / 5 / 4 / 4 items for Basic
through Legendary; field goods require 16 / 14 / 12 / 10 / 9 / 8. Storing or
moving an item through a bag or chest does not improve its quality.

Coin purchases draw from **your inventory first, then the bound Hearth, then
valid linked Warehouse chests/barrels**. A loose chest or Lumber Camp chest is
not automatically a treasury.

Next, open Growth: **Warehouse (2 Coins)** after a real camp log; hire a
**Courier (2)** and complete a delivery; **Cultivated Ground (2)** for the
Farmhouse plan and **Farmer (2)**. **Home (2)** requires Shelter plus
Foundation Ready and unlocks House and Lodging; it does not wait for a Farmer
crop. Then learn **Hospitality (4)** after Home and three housed settlers, and
buy **Innkeeper (2)**. Read each live node requirement before spending.

**Recruit when ready.** A valid Tavern, housing, morale and ready meals attract
a real traveler during ordinary play. A valid Tavern includes all Plaque
requirements, including a connected Ale Tap and barrel. New quotes start at
4/6/8 Coins before any fixed discount; pay the exact card price. Recruitment
also needs a genuinely free bed and eight ready meals per resident after
joining. A seated visitor is still a visitor, and paying for a Tavern meal does
not recruit them. No Trader profession is required for manual merchant trades.
