# Bannerhold: more small event ideas (for the owner to approve)

None of these is built. Each would plug into the same director as the 8 approved events: one per day at most, quiet days, never near a raid, only while someone is online, and a clean end.
**[cheap]** = mostly reuses systems that already exist (the talk UI, settler roles, vanilla mobs, the tavern, Coins, the Warehouse).

| # | Event | What happens | Your choice | Reward / cost |
|---|-------|--------------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Lost child** [cheap] | A settler's child wanders into the woods at dusk, and a marker shows the last place anyone saw them. | Search yourself, send a guard (who leaves the walls), or wait till morning. | Found: big morale boost for the family. Waiting too long risks wolves and a sad village. |
| 2 | **Bard's rumour** [cheap] | A traveller in the tavern sings of a buried chest nearby, and a map mark appears. | Dig it up yourself, or pay 3 Coins for "the second verse", which gives the exact spot. | A loot chest (Coins, a rare tool). Sometimes it's a trap with 2 skeleton bandits. |
| 3 | **Stray dog** [cheap] — APPROVED, BUILT 26 Sep | A scruffy dog follows your settlers around. | Feed it (1 meat): it stays and barks when raid scouts come near. Or shoo it away. | An earlier raid warning while the dog lives. |
| 4 | **Fog night traveller** [cheap] | Thick fog, and a lost merchant knocks at the Banner asking the way. | Guide him (walk him to the edge), give him a bed for the night, or ignore him. | Guiding him: he comes back later as a friendly trader with a discount. |
| 5 | **Harvest moon feast** [cheap] | The night after a big harvest, the village wants a feast. | Spend 20 food on a feast in the dining hall or tavern, or save the food. | A feast is +morale for everyone for a day. Saving the food is a small grumble. |
| 6 | **First snow** [cheap] | The first snow of winter (tied to seasons later). Children play and guards shiver. | Hand out wool or cloaks (10 wool), or let them be cold. | Warm: no work slowdown. Cold: slower workers for a day. |
| 7 | **Falling star** | A meteor lands just outside the claim, smoking. | Send a miner to dig it out before the neighbours' scouts do, or leave it. | Rare ore (iron and gold, maybe a blessing seal). Scouts may show up. |
| 8 | **Hermit at the edge** [cheap] | An old hermit sets up camp just outside the walls. | Invite him in (he becomes a healer or scholar), trade herbs with him, or run him off. | A free skilled settler, or potions. Running him off lowers morale. |
| 9 | **Passing caravan** [cheap] — APPROVED, BUILT 26 Sep | A small caravan of carts rolls along the nearest road and doesn't stop. | Hail them (pay 2 Coins toll to open the market), escort them (guards leave for a while), or let them pass. | A big Coin trade window. Escorting them earns their favour: cheaper goods next time. |
| 10 | **Sick settler** [cheap] | One settler falls ill and can't work, and it may spread. | Pay a healer, use the infirmary, or quarantine them in their house. | Healed quickly: no spread. Ignored: 1-2 more settlers ill for a day. |
| 11 | **Wedding** [cheap] | Two settlers want to marry in the tavern. | Bless it and pay for the feast (Coins plus food), or say "not now". | Big morale lift. Later, a family with a child who inherits a trade (the relationships system). |
| 12 | **Rival envoy** [cheap] — APPROVED, BUILT 26 Sep | An envoy from a rival kingdom arrives with a message (the diplomacy hook). | Receive them with a gift, insult them, or hold them hostage. | Relation with the rival goes up or down. Hostage: ransom Coins, but a raid is certain. |
| 13 | **Deserter** [cheap] | A wounded raider stumbles in and asks to switch sides. | Take him in (a new guard, but can you trust him?), hand him over, or finish him. | A free veteran guard. Small chance he's a spy who opens the gate on the next raid. |
| 14 | **Bees in the orchard** [cheap] | A wild bee nest appears in a tree by the fields. | Smoke it out and take the honey (a campfire under it), or let it be. | Honey and a beehive. Left alone, the fields grow faster for 3 days. |
| 15 | **Flooded field** | A heavy rain night turns part of a farm into mud. | Settlers dig drainage (a work task, a day of lost farming), or leave it. | Drained: fields back to normal and better next rain. Left: that plot yields half for 2 days. |
| 16 | **Pilgrims to the shrine** | Pilgrims walk through asking to pray at your tallest tower or shrine. | Let them in (they buy food with Coins), charge a toll, or close the gate. | Coins and a small blessing chance. Closing the gate: bad reputation with travellers. |
| 17 | **Travelling smith** [cheap] | A smith offers to repair or upgrade guard gear for one day. | Pay Coins per piece, or trade iron for the work. | Gear upgraded a tier for fewer Coins than crafting it. |
| 18 | **Horse thief at night** | A thief tries to lead away a horse or a cow from the pasture. | Catch him (chase), let the guards handle it, or offer him work. | Caught: a Coin fine. Given work: a new, shady settler. |
| 19 | **Storyteller's night** [cheap] | An old settler tells a raid story by the Banner fire and everyone gathers. | Join (sit by the fire) or keep working. | Guards gain a little XP. The village is braver in the next raid (less fleeing). |
| 20 | **Bandit ambush on a courier** | Your cart or courier is stopped on the road by 2 bandits. | Ride out and rescue them, pay the bandits off, or let the cargo go. | Rescue: cargo and bandit loot. Paying costs Coins. Letting it go loses the goods. |

**My top 5 to build next (cheap, and each makes the world feel bigger):** 3 stray dog, 9 passing caravan, 12 rival envoy, 1 lost child, 5 harvest moon feast.
