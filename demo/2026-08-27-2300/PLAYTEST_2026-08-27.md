## Recommended first test

Create a fresh Creative world with commands enabled. The gameplay is still server-authoritative; Creative mode simply removes gathering time from this QA round.

1. Run `/hearthstead demo`. This gives the first Hearth, House Build Plan, Handbook and basic supplies.
2. Place the Hearth on open ground. Three settlers should arrive and the settlement should be founded.
3. Right-click the Hearth. Open **Journey** and confirm that **Light the first fire** is complete. Do **not** press **Skip journey** if you want to reach the authored first raid.
4. Open **Development** at the Hearth. **Settlement Charter** should be learned and the House plan should be available.
5. Build a closed House with at least nine interior floor blocks, one door, light and **two beds**. Hang a Plaque facing into the room and use the **House Build Plan** on it. Open the Plaque and use **Requirements** if anything is missing.
6. Open **Mayor** at the Hearth and appoint one living settler.
7. Open the House Plaque and assign two settlers as residents. This is required for the next development milestone.
8. Put **8 logs and 8 cobblestone** into the Hearth storage. Open **Development**, select **Shelter**, then press **Learn this knowledge**.
9. Put **12 logs and 4 leather** into the Hearth storage. Once two settlers are housed, learn **Timber Rights**. This teaches the Lumber Camp plan and unlocks Lumberer Emblems.
10. Craft the Lumber Camp plan shapeless from **paper + feather + any log**. A Plaque is crafted from six copper ingots, one iron ingot and three planks.
11. Build a closed Lumber Camp with at least sixteen interior floor blocks, one crafting table, one chest or barrel, one door and one light. Hang a blank Plaque facing into the room, then use the Lumber Camp plan on it. The Plaque must show **Registered**.
12. Put **2 flint and 2 leather** into the Hearth storage. Empty-hand right-click the appointed Mayor and buy one **Lumberer Emblem**.
13. Open the Lumber Camp Plaque, choose **Hire**, and hire an unemployed settler. The physical emblem must be consumed by this exact hire. The admin command `/hearthstead hire` deliberately does not count toward the Journey or first-raid proof.
14. Let the new Lumberer create an equipment request, then place a real axe in the Lumber Camp chest or barrel. The worker must receive and use a physical tool; workers no longer spawn with free equipment.
15. Make sure a natural tree is inside the settlement radius. During work hours the Lumberer should fell it, put the sack down, collect the physical drops one at a time, stow them, lift the sack and deliver the logs to Lumber Camp storage.
16. Reopen **Journey** at the Hearth. **Bank the first log** should complete only after that hired Lumberer stores a real log in the correct camp.

## First raid

The first raid is not a button or an admin-spawned substitute. Under the default profile it is scheduled **four to seven nights after founding**, with a warning **two nights before**. The server rechecks all requirements when warning and attack begin.

Required proof:

- A real founded and still-bound Hearth.
- A living appointed Mayor.
- A valid House and valid Lumber Camp.
- Settlement Charter, Shelter and Timber Rights learned.
- Founding Journey completed without using Skip.
- A living Lumberer hired using a real Mayor-purchased emblem.
- A real log stored by that exact Lumberer in that exact Lumber Camp.

For a time-compressed QA run, `/time add 24000` advances one Minecraft day. Use it only after the requirements above are genuinely complete. The same raid plan and identity must survive warning, refusal and retry; do not replace the real readiness steps with admin hiring.

## Controls worth testing

- **Right-click Hearth:** settlement storage and tabs for Mayor, Journey and Development.
- **Empty-hand right-click Mayor:** Job Emblem shop.
- **Shift + right-click any settler:** settler sheet and real inventory.
- **Shift + right-click a Courier:** settler sheet, then **Request Queue**. Requests are numbered in their real server order and can be dragged to reprioritize them.
- **Right-click a registered Plaque:** Requirements, People/Hire and building controls.
- **Shift + right-click with a Blessing Seal:** permanently bind that blessing to a settler or eligible Plaque target.

## What to watch and report

- Any UI that pauses or drops FPS when opening, dragging or scrolling.
- Plaques that blink, disappear or show the wrong plan.
- Every Lumberer state: chopping, sack down, walking to a drop, pickup contact, visible offhand carry, stow contact, sack up, laden walk and camp deposit.
- A worker receiving or using a tool without a real item transfer.
- A Courier delivering requests out of the displayed order.
- A Guard attacking without a real weapon, failing to protect a civilian, or receiving XP more than once for one kill.
- The exact screen, action and approximate time if anything fails. A short recording is ideal.
