# Recommended optional mods

Hearthstead is **fully standalone** — none of these are required. But if you
want the settlement to feel even more alive, these Minecraft **1.20.1 Forge**
mods pair well with it. Install them yourself from CurseForge or Modrinth;
no third-party files are bundled or redistributed here.

| Mod | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| **Xaero's Minimap** | See your settlers and settlement surroundings at a glance; waypoint the hearth. |
| **Xaero's World Map** | A full map of the lands your settlement will one day claim. |
| **Embeddium** | Rendering performance — keeps things smooth with many entities working at once. |
| **Oculus** | Shader support (pairs with Embeddium). Warm amber hearth-light at dusk with shaders is *the* Hearthstead look. |
| **Jade** | Hover tooltips on blocks/entities; complements the settler nameplates and hearth screen. |
| **JEI (Just Enough Items)** | Look up the hearth, writ, and handbook recipes in-game. |
| **AmbientSounds** | Birdsong, wind, and night ambience around the settlement. |
| **Sound Physics Remastered** | Reverb and occlusion — the chop of the lumberer's axe echoing off a hillside sells the world. |

Notes:

- Get the **1.20.1 Forge** build of each mod; some of them also ship Fabric
  builds that will not work here.
- Embeddium + Oculus replace OptiFine; do not mix them with OptiFine.
- Hearthstead does **not** require MineColonies, TekTopia, or any other
  settlement mod — and has no known conflicts with the mods above.
